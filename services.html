01 · Prevention

Preventive cardiology & risk assessment

Cardiovascular risk profiling

Assessment of personal cardiovascular risk using relevant medical and family history, lifestyle factors, and investigations.

Risk-factor review

Clinical evaluation of factors such as high blood pressure, cholesterol, diabetes, smoking, and weight.

Lifestyle & activity guidance

Individualized conversations about physical activity, nutrition, weight management, and long-term risk follow-up.

02 · Diagnostics

Non-invasive cardiac investigations

12-lead electrocardiography (ECG)

A recording of the heart’s electrical activity to support assessment of rhythm and other cardiac findings.

Adult 2D echocardiography

Ultrasound imaging to assess heart chambers, wall motion, valve function, and related measures.

Advanced strain imaging (GLS)

Specialized echocardiographic imaging to assess myocardial function as part of a clinical evaluation.

Ambulatory Holter monitoring

Extended ECG recording that can help assess intermittent palpitations and other episodic symptoms.

Treadmill stress test (TMT)

Assessment of the heart’s response to exercise in a supervised diagnostic setting when clinically appropriate.

03 · Specialized evaluation

Additional cardiac assessments

Cardio-oncology assessment

Cardiac evaluation and monitoring for people undergoing or recovering from potentially heart-affecting cancer treatment.

Pre-exercise cardiac assessment

Cardiovascular evaluation before beginning high-intensity or structured exercise, based on individual risk.

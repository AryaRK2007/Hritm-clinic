Frequently asked questions

A few things you may be wondering.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring previous medical reports and test results, a list of medicines you take, and any questions you would like to discuss. Sharing relevant family and medical history can also help your doctor understand your concerns.

Do I need a referral before booking?

Referral requirements can vary. Please check with the clinic when arranging your visit if you have a referral or specific paperwork requirement.

Will every consultation include a diagnostic test?

Not necessarily. The doctor will discuss your symptoms and history, then decide whether an investigation is appropriate for your individual situation.

How should I prepare for a cardiac test?

Preparation depends on the test. Follow any instructions provided by the clinic when your test is arranged, and ask beforehand if anything is unclear.

Can I use the online test finder to diagnose a condition?

No. The test finder offers general examples only. It cannot diagnose a condition or determine which test is right for you. Please speak with a qualified clinician for personal medical advice.

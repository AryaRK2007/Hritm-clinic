Before you arrive

A few helpful reminders.

  • Bring previous reports, test results, and a current medication list.
  • Note when your symptoms began and what tends to bring them on.
  • Write down questions you would like to discuss with the doctor.
  • Follow any specific instructions provided when your appointment is confirmed.

Need urgent care?

Hri-thm Clinic is for planned consultations and non-invasive diagnostics. For severe chest pain, sudden breathlessness, or fainting, seek immediate help at the nearest hospital emergency department.
